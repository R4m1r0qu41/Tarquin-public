# Ley de Firma Electrónica Avanzada (MX) art. 7 — Efectos

## Summary

Art. 7 provides that a *firma electrónica avanzada* may be used in electronic documents and,
where applicable, in mensajes de datos, and that documents and mensajes de datos bearing a
firma electrónica avanzada **produce the same effects as those presented with a handwritten
(autograph) signature and, consequently, have the same probative value** the applicable
provisions grant the latter. Art. 8 conditions those effects on the firma meeting the guiding
principles — equivalencia funcional, autenticidad, integridad, neutralidad tecnológica, and no
repudio.

## Why it matters here

This is the federal-administration counterpart to the Código de Comercio commercial regime: it
pins the legal effect and probative value of an *advanced* electronic signature for federal
acts. A verification attestation that asserts a Mexican advanced-electronic-signature document
is effective should cite this node together with [[lex-mx-ccom-89]] (commercial) and identify
which regime governs the document at hand.
