# 17 CFR § 230.902 — Regulation S: Definitions

## Summary

The defined-term backbone of Regulation S:

- **(f) Distribution compliance period** — runs from the later of first offering to
  persons other than distributors or the closing, for the duration § 230.903 specifies
  per category.
- **(h) Offshore transaction** — the buyer is (or is reasonably believed to be) outside
  the United States at origination, or the transaction executes on a designated offshore
  securities market without pre-arrangement with a US buyer.
- **(k) U.S. person** — natural persons resident in the US; US-organized partnerships
  and corporations; certain estates, trusts, agency accounts; and foreign entities
  formed by a U.S. person principally to invest in unregistered securities. Paragraph
  (k)(2) lists exclusions (e.g. certain foreign branches/agencies, international
  organizations).
- Also: **directed selling efforts** (c), **distributor** (d), **designated offshore
  securities market** (b), **substantial U.S. market interest** (j), **debt securities**
  (a), **reporting issuer** (i).
