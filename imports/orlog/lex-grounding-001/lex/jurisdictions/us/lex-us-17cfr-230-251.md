# 17 CFR § 230.251 — Regulation A: Scope of exemption

## Summary

Head of Regulation A (§§ 230.251–230.263), the exemption built on Securities Act
§ 3(b). Two tiers, defined by 12-month aggregate offering limits:

- **Tier 1:** up to **$20,000,000**, including not more than $6,000,000 by selling
  securityholders that are affiliates.
- **Tier 2:** up to **$75,000,000**, including not more than $22,500,000 by selling
  securityholders that are affiliates.

Eligibility conditions (issuer type, disqualifications) and ongoing requirements live in
the following sections (230.252–263), not yet mapped as nodes.
