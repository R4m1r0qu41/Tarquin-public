# 17 CFR § 230.502 — Regulation D: General conditions

## Summary

Four general conditions for Regulation D offerings:

- **(a) Integration** — defers to § 230.152 for whether separate offerings are treated
  as one.
- **(b) Information** — specified disclosure to purchasers that are not accredited
  investors a reasonable time before sale, scaled by issuer reporting status and
  offering size.
- **(c) Manner of offering** — no **general solicitation or general advertising**
  (subject to stated exceptions); Rule 506(c) separately lifts this for
  all-accredited, verified offerings.
- **(d) Limitations on resale** — securities acquired in a Reg D offering have
  **restricted-securities** status: resale requires registration or an exemption, and
  the issuer must exercise reasonable care that purchasers are not statutory
  underwriters (legends, stop-transfer, purchaser representations).

## Why it matters here

(d) is the legal substance behind `prim-restricted-transfer`: transfer-restriction
clauses in Reg D notes and subscription artifacts cite this node. (c) is recorded as a
declared offering fact in the Reg D representation artifact.
