# New York UCC § 2-718 — Liquidation or limitation of damages; deposits

## Summary

New York UCC § 2-718 permits contractual liquidation of damages for breach only at an
amount reasonable in light of anticipated or actual harm, difficulty of proof of loss,
and inconvenience or nonfeasibility of obtaining an adequate remedy. A term fixing
unreasonably large liquidated damages is void as a penalty. The section also addresses
buyer restitution when a seller justifiably withholds delivery because of buyer breach.

## Why it matters here

This is the New York statutory basis for `prim-late-fee-interest` in goods-style late
payment and late delivery artifacts. The arithmetic remains deterministic; whether a
rate or per-day charge is reasonable under the statute remains a legal judgment and is
