# 17 CFR § 230.901 — Regulation S: General statement

## Summary

The territorial principle of Regulation S in one sentence: for purposes of Securities
Act § 5 ([[lex-us-15usc-77e]]), the terms "offer," "offer to sell," "sell," "sale," and
"offer to buy" include offers and sales **within the United States** and exclude those
**outside the United States**. Rules 903 and 904 are the issuer and resale safe harbors
that make "outside the United States" determinable.

## Why it matters here

The root of `tax-securities.offshore-offering.reg-s` and the framing rule for
`slc-reg-s-offshore-restriction-001`. The artifact represents and restricts; whether a
transaction is in fact "outside the United States" beyond the safe-harbor conditions is
a legal judgment.
