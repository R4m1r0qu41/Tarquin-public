# 15 U.S.C. § 80a-3 — Investment Company Act § 3: Definition; private-fund exclusions

## Summary

Defines "investment company" and carves out exclusions in § 80a-3(c):

- **§ 3(c)(1):** an issuer whose outstanding securities (other than short-term paper)
  are beneficially owned by not more than **100 persons** (or **250** for a qualifying
  venture capital fund) and which is not making and does not presently propose to make a
  **public offering** — excluded from the definition. (Still deemed an investment
  company for certain anti-pyramiding limits on acquiring registered fund securities.)
- **§ 3(c)(7):** an issuer whose outstanding securities are owned exclusively by
  **qualified purchasers** ([[lex-us-15usc-80a-2]]) and which is not making and does not
  presently propose to make a public offering — excluded. Grandfathering permits limited
  pre-1996 non-qualified holders under disclosure and redemption-offer conditions.

## Why it matters here

`tax-investment-company.exemptions.private-fund` artifacts are representation
checklists for these two exclusions: owner counts and qualified-purchaser status as
declared facts. Beneficial-ownership counting rules (look-through, knowledgeable
employees under 17 CFR 270.3c-5) involve legal judgment — artifacts flag, never decide.
