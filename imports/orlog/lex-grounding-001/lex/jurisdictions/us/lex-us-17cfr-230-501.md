# 17 CFR § 230.501 — Regulation D: Definitions (accredited investor)

## Summary

Rule 501(a) defines **accredited investor** in thirteen categories, including:

- (a)(1) banks, registered brokers/dealers, investment advisers, insurance companies and
  similar institutions; (a)(2) private business development companies; (a)(3) certain
  entities with total assets over $5,000,000 not formed for the purpose of the
  investment;
- (a)(4) directors, executive officers, or general partners of the issuer;
- (a)(5) natural persons with **individual (or joint with spouse/spousal-equivalent) net
  worth over $1,000,000**, excluding primary residence per the calculation rules;
- (a)(6) natural persons with **income over $200,000** in each of the two most recent
  years (**$300,000 joint**) and a reasonable expectation of the same;
- (a)(7) trusts over $5,000,000 directed by a sophisticated person; (a)(8) entities all
  of whose equity owners are accredited; (a)(9) entities owning over $5,000,000 in
  investments; (a)(10) holders of Commission-designated professional certifications;
  (a)(11) knowledgeable employees of private funds; (a)(12)–(13) qualifying family
  offices and their family clients.

The definition includes persons the issuer **reasonably believes** fall within a
category at the time of sale.
