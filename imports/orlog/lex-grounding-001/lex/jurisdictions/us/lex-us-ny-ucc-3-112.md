# New York UCC § 3-112 — Terms and omissions not affecting negotiability

## Summary

New York UCC § 3-112 states that negotiability is not affected by specified omissions
or terms, including omission of consideration or place of drawing/payment, collateral
statements, promises or powers to maintain or protect collateral, confession-of-judgment
terms, certain waivers, satisfaction language in drafts, set-of-parts language, choice
of governing law, and forum-selection undertakings. It also states that the section
does not validate any term otherwise illegal.
