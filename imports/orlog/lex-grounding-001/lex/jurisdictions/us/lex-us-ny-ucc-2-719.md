# New York UCC § 2-719 — Contractual modification or limitation of remedy

## Summary

New York UCC § 2-719 permits agreements to provide remedies in addition to or in
substitution for Article 2 remedies and to limit or alter the measure of damages.
Resort to an agreed remedy is optional unless expressly made exclusive, in which case it
is the sole remedy. If an exclusive or limited remedy fails of its essential purpose,
Article 2 remedies remain available. Consequential damages may be limited or excluded
unless the limitation or exclusion is unconscionable.

## Why it matters here

This is the New York statutory basis for `prim-remedy-cap` and exclusive-remedy wording
in late-payment and late-delivery artifacts. The SLCs can compute the cap; they do not
decide essential-purpose failure or unconscionability.
