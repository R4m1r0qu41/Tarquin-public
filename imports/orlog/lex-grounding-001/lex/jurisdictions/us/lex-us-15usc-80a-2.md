# 15 U.S.C. § 80a-2 — Investment Company Act § 2: Definitions (qualified purchaser)

## Summary

§ 80a-2(a)(51)(A) defines **qualified purchaser** in four categories:

1. natural persons owning not less than **$5,000,000 in investments** (including certain
   joint/spousal holdings);
2. family-owned companies with not less than **$5,000,000 in investments** owned by two
   or more related natural persons (or their estates, trusts, charitable vehicles);
3. trusts not formed for the specific purpose of acquiring the securities, where each
   trustee/settlor is a person in category 1, 2, or 4;
4. persons acting for their own account or other qualified purchasers' accounts who own
   and invest on a discretionary basis not less than **$25,000,000 in investments**.

## Why it matters here

The § 3(c)(7) private-fund exclusion ([[lex-us-15usc-80a-3]]) requires every owner to be
a qualified purchaser. Eligibility-representation primitives carry this as a declared,
threshold-checkable fact; whether assets count as "investments" is defined in SEC rules
(17 CFR 270.2a51-1) and is not decided by artifacts.
