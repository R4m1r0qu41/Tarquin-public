# 17 CFR § 230.904 — Regulation S: Resale safe harbor

## Summary

Resales by persons other than the issuer/distributors/affiliates qualify when made in an
**offshore transaction** with **no directed selling efforts** in the United States.
Additional conditions apply to dealers and persons receiving selling concessions
(no knowledge that the buyer is a U.S. person; notice to purchasing dealers during the
distribution compliance period) and to officers/directors who are affiliates solely by
office (no special remuneration beyond usual broker's commission).

## Why it matters here

The resale-restriction clause in `slc-reg-s-offshore-restriction-001` requires the
holder to resell only in compliance with this rule, registration, or another exemption.
