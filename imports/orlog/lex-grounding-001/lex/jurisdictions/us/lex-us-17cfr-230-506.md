# 17 CFR § 230.506 — Regulation D: Rule 506 safe harbors

## Summary

Safe harbor under Securities Act § 4(a)(2) ([[lex-us-15usc-77d]]), unlimited in dollar
amount:

- **506(b):** no general solicitation; no more than **35 non-accredited purchasers**
  within a 90-calendar-day period, each of whom (alone or with a purchaser
  representative) has — or the issuer reasonably believes has — the knowledge and
  experience to evaluate the investment; §§ 230.501/230.502 conditions apply.
- **506(c):** general solicitation permitted if **all purchasers are accredited
  investors** and the issuer takes **reasonable steps to verify** that status —
  non-exclusive verification methods include IRS-form income review, net-worth
  documentation, written confirmation from a registered broker-dealer, SEC-registered
  investment adviser, licensed attorney, or CPA, and reliance on a prior verification
  within five years.
