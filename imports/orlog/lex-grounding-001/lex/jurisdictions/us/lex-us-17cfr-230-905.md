# 17 CFR § 230.905 — Regulation S: Resale limitations

## Summary

Equity securities of **domestic issuers** acquired from the issuer, a distributor, or
their affiliates in a Regulation S transaction are **restricted securities** within the
meaning of § 230.144. Resale must be under Regulation S, registration, or another
exemption — and restricted status survives a Regulation S resale.

## Why it matters here

With § 230.502(d), the legal substance behind `prim-restricted-transfer`: the
"restricted status survives" rule is why transfer-restriction clauses must propagate to
transferees, which is a representable (and partially computable) artifact obligation.
