# 15 U.S.C. § 77e — Securities Act of 1933 § 5: Registration prohibition

## Summary

The core prohibition the exemptions orbit. § 77e(a): unlawful to sell or deliver an
unregistered security through interstate commerce or the mails unless a registration
statement is in effect. § 77e(c): unlawful to offer to sell or buy through those
channels unless a registration statement has been filed (and is not subject to refusal
or stop order).

## Why it matters here

Every exemption artifact in `slc/` (Reg A, Reg D, Reg S, § 4(a)(2)) is a posture
*against this section*. Regulation S (Rule 901) limits this section's terms "offer,
offer to sell, sell, sale, offer to buy" to transactions inside the United States.
