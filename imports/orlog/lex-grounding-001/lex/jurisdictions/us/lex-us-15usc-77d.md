# 15 U.S.C. § 77d — Securities Act of 1933 § 4: Exempted transactions

## Summary

Lists transactions exempt from § 77e (§ 5) registration. The pivotal provision for this
repo is **§ 77d(a)(2)**: "transactions by an issuer not involving any public offering" —
the statutory private-offering exemption. Regulation D's Rule 506 is the SEC safe harbor
under it.

## Why it matters here

`slc/slc-reg-d-investor-representation-001` exists because issuers relying on
§ 77d(a)(2) / Rule 506 need investor representations recorded as structured, checkable
facts. The artifact records representations; it does not determine that an offering is
non-public.
