# New York UCC § 3-104 — Negotiable instruments; note

## Summary

New York UCC § 3-104 provides that a negotiable instrument must be signed, contain an
unconditional promise or order to pay a sum certain in money and no other promise/order
except as authorized by Article 3, be payable on demand or at a definite time, and be
payable to order or bearer. It classifies a compliant promise, other than a certificate
of deposit, as a "note."
