# 17 CFR § 230.903 — Regulation S: Issuer safe harbor

## Summary

Two general conditions for issuer-side offers/sales: (1) the offer or sale is made in an
**offshore transaction**; (2) **no directed selling efforts** in the United States by
the issuer, distributors, affiliates, or persons acting on their behalf. Then three
categories add conditions:

- **Category 1** — no further conditions (foreign issuers with no substantial U.S.
  market interest, overseas directed offerings, foreign-government-backed securities,
  certain employee benefit plans).
- **Category 2** — offering restrictions, a **40-day** distribution compliance period,
  and distributor notices (equity of reporting foreign issuers; debt of reporting
  issuers and non-reporting foreign issuers).
- **Category 3** — everything else: offering restrictions, **40-day (debt) / 1-year
  (equity)** distribution compliance periods, purchaser certifications (not a U.S.
  person, offshore transaction), contractual transfer restrictions, legends, and
  distributor notices.

## Why it matters here

The category and its conditions are the core declared facts in
`slc-reg-s-offshore-restriction-001`; the distribution-compliance-period arithmetic is
the only mechanically computable piece (dates), and the artifact computes only that.
