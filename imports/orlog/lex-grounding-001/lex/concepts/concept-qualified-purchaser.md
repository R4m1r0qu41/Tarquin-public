# concept: Qualified purchaser

Defined at [[lex-us-15usc-80a-2]] (a)(51)(A): $5,000,000-in-investments natural persons
and family companies; qualifying trusts; and $25,000,000 discretionary institutional
investors. What counts as "investments" is specified by SEC rule (17 CFR 270.2a51-1),
not mapped yet.

**Effect.** Gates the § 3(c)(7) private-fund exclusion ([[lex-us-15usc-80a-3]]): every
beneficial owner must be a qualified purchaser. Much higher bar than
[[concept-accredited-investor]].

**Artifact treatment.** Declared status plus threshold facts; "investments" valuation
judgments stay human.
