# concept: Offshore transaction

Defined at [[lex-us-17cfr-230-902]] (h): the offer is not made to a person in the United
States, and either (a) at origination of the buy order the buyer is (or is reasonably
believed to be) outside the United States, or (b) the transaction executes on a
designated offshore securities market without pre-arrangement with a buyer in the
United States.

**Effect.** General condition (1) of both Reg S safe harbors
([[lex-us-17cfr-230-903]], resale rule 904).

**Artifact treatment.** Location/market facts as declarations; "reasonably believed" is
human territory.
